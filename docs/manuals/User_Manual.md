# Platform User Manual

Welcome to the Digital Publication Platform. This guide will help you navigate, read, and manage your account.

## 1. Finding Content
- **Global Search**: Use the search bar at the top of the dashboard. Powered by our NLP engine, you can search for exact titles, topics, or keywords extracted from within the documents.
- **Library**: Browse by Categories, Genres, or Collections.
- **Recommendations**: Based on your reading history, the "For You" section provides daily updated book recommendations.

## 2. Reading Publications
Our advanced PDF Reader allows you to read heavy publications seamlessly.
- **Controls**: Use the bottom toolbar to Zoom, switch to Fullscreen, or toggle Dark Mode.
- **Bookmarks**: Click the Ribbon icon to save your current page. Bookmarks are synchronized across all your devices.
- **Streaming**: You do not need to wait for a 100MB PDF to download. The reader instantly streams only the pages you are looking at.

## 3. Managing Subscriptions
- Navigate to **Profile > Subscription**.
- View your active tier (Free, Basic, Premium).
- Click **Manage Billing** to update your credit card or cancel your subscription securely via Stripe.
